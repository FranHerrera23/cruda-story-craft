Moment New entity Sector Architecture Where Salta, Argentina Years 2020–2022 The moment Germán Noel had built Cristalizando into the leading glass and high-performance openings manufacturer in northern Argentina: an industrial plant, façades for hospitals and towers, contracts won on volume and price. INOUT was the opposite. A frameless system he engineered himself — 20mm vertical profiles, insulated glass — quoted project by project, for houses that wanted the wall to disappear. The narrative problem He was already known. That was the problem. Known as the reliable supplier, and that reputation does not travel up to the tier where studios are buying authorship rather than specification. He was already known. That was the problem. Vos elegís de qué lado estar. You choose which side to be on. Launched in August 2021, with half the world still shut indoors. The line turns inside and outside from a condition into a choice. The lines trace an opening and, at the centre, a camera lens: the brand&#x27;s two axes, contemplation and movement, in a single figure. The palette runs from electric blue to cement green because the thesis is industrial architecture plus sky and nature. That is not execution. That is the argument of the brand resolved as a system. INSIDERS The format: the brand interviewing the architects who decide what gets built in the region. INOUT never explains its own quality. It hosts the people whose judgment sets it. This is where the method starts. Five years on, INSIDERS is still in production — by the client, without us. A system that outlasts the engagement proves more than one that requires it. INSIDERS are the ones who dream awake. The mad ones who know they are mad. The ones who contemplate , not the ones who look. The ones who turn up the volume on silence. The ones who slow time down. The ones who make wherever they are their place.
