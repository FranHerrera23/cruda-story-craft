01 / A proposal for Mike

Bring the right clients in.
Help the work succeed.

You want paying clients and a lean team.
I’d like to help build that with you, under the ZUY name.

Why us.

We’ve worked together at Norhart. You know how I think; I know the work behind your story. I now work closely with owners in construction, design and other established businesses.

What I would bring.

Client relationships, a clear brief and a design system for what we put in front of them. I would help shape and review the experience; your team and AI build it.

The aim: more of the right work, a clearer start for each project and clients who stay.

A performance-based partnership to explore. The following pages show how brand, design and sales can support it.

One standard for the website,
client products and internal tools.

We agree the patterns once. Your team and AI reuse them as they build. I review the experience with you before it reaches the client.

Make the important thing clear.

Parts.

Buttons, forms, tables and dashboards.
Familiar parts across tools.

Behavior.

Loading, missing data, errors and review.
Make the next step clear.
